Signal Breakdown

What actually gets checked

Every signal below runs on every scan. See the full breakdown →

01

ML Model

A neural network trained on ~18,000 real postings scores the TF-IDF vectorized text for fraud probability.

02

Scam-Pattern Rules

Known scam phrasing (fees, wire transfers, WhatsApp-only contact) weighted by severity, not an auto-override.

03

Vagueness Check

Flags postings that are too thin: no named company, no real way to apply, unusually short.

04

AI Reasoning (optional)

When configured, an open-weight LLM (Llama 3.3 via Groq) adds its own fraud score and plain-English explanation.
